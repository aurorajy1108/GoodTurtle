The student deliberately issued this instruction, and the employee deliberately carried it out. The misunderstanding was not that the courses had been wrongly enrolled, was not a joke or hypothetical, and was not intended to transfer the spots to anyone; the student did not use the enrollment system personally or alter data, and the courses were not cancelled, filled by competitors, or erased through a lost account/credentials.

The explanation I submit is: while at a counter on campus, the student asked the staff member to process a course-enrollment change, intending to register/add courses. The employee misinterpreted the request as a request to remove the student's courses and executed a drop instead. As a result, by the end of that evening all of the student's selected courses had been removed from their record — the courses were "gone" not because the system failed or someone sabotaged them, but because the student's own spoken instruction, misunderstood at the counter, caused the staff member to drop every course.
