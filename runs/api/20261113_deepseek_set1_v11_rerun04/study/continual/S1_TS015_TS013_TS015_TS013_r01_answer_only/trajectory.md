The wordplay is on "courses." They are not academic classes but the successive dishes of a banquet meal, and the "deadline" is the advance-ordering deadline for that dinner. The student, a guest at a formal multi-course banquet that evening, had successfully pre-selected their dishes before the ordering cutoff. But when the meal was served, the student voluntarily passed all of their own pre-selected courses to a particular person they knew at the table, because for their own reasons the student could not or would not eat those dishes themselves. The servers then cleared the plates as the meal progressed, and the student sat in place throughout. So by the end of the single evening, every course that had been chosen/reserved for the student had been given away and taken off the table — "all of my courses were gone." The academic framing (HKU student, choosing courses before a deadline) is a decoy; the reality is a banquet where the student gave away each of their food courses to someone else, emptying their place at the table in one evening.
