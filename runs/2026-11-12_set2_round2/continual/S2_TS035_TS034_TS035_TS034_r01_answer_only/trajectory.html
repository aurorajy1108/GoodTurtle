62 · final

The man on the phone is the father, and the "hostage" whose life the kidnapper threatens is the father himself, not his daughter. The kidnapper has seized the father at his own location and is keeping him there captive while forcing the phone call; the daughter is elsewhere, desperately begging the kidnapper to spare her father's life. The kidnapper demands that 100 million yen be brought alone by noon, with no police.

The father decides the ransom should not be paid for two linked reasons. First, he believes the kidnapper will kill him regardless of whether the money is paid—the ransom would not save his life. Second, the only way the ransom could be delivered is by his daughter bringing the money alone, which would expose her to grave danger from the kidnapper. Paying would therefore gain nothing for him while endangering the person he most wants to protect.

So rather than let his daughter walk into danger to pay a ransom that will not save him, the father chooses to sacrifice his own life and refuses to have the ransom paid.
