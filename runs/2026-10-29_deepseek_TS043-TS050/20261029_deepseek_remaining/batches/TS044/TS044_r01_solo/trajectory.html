53 · final

The narrator's error was not about the cat's identity, existence, death, loyalty, or role in exposing the crime. The black cat is a real living pet that belonged to the narrator. The narrator had believed the cat was not accompanying or watching him—that it had stopped following him—but the cat had in fact trailed after him and was now watching him from the doorway as the police led him away. Seeing the cat there corrected that belief: the cat had followed/accompanied him after all. The killing itself was intentional, but it was not committed to protect the cat, because of harm to the cat, or because the cat revealed anything; those branches are excluded.
