## JP001 — 難問と、答え合わせ

[日语来源](https://late-late.jp/mondai/show/13003)；作者：ドラノール１２ (opening: きの子)。

**English opening**

At school, A caused classmate B to transfer elsewhere. They met again at university, began dating, and married after starting work. Everyone thought they were a loving couple. Years later B disappeared, and A was arrested on suspicion of murdering B. What happened between them?

**English solution (private)**

A had photographed a murder by B's parent and reported it, leading to arrest and B's relocation to relatives. A felt guilty. A's surname later changed after parental divorce and remarriage. At university A recognized B, but B did not recognize A. They fell in love and married, each hiding the past. Years later B recognized A, wrongly believed A had not recognized B, and fled in shame over being a murderer's child, leaving divorce papers and an apology. B had not learned that A had reported the original crime. Unable to find B, A fabricated evidence and confessed to B's murder, hoping news would reach B, convey acceptance of B's past, and bring B back to clear A. B was alive; A had not killed B.

**翻译与边界说明**

Concise English translation/paraphrase of opening and causal narrative, not a line-by-line translation of lengthy monologues. A/B preserved. Original human thread had many hints and 69 questions; that is not our agent count. No wordplay required. Outcome that B actually returns is not required: final story leaves hope, although source accepted QA includes return.

## JP002 — 犯人と暮らした一週間

[日语来源](https://late-late.jp/mondai/show/18149)；作者：うつま。

**English opening**

A man was kidnapped and spent a week with his captor. Police stormed the location, shot the captor dead, and freed him. Afterwards concerned friends contacted him. He assured them he was fine but would never tell them what had happened that week. Why?

**English solution (private)**

His friends were people he knew only through online gaming. After a week offline, he logged in and they worried he had been ill. He wanted to keep his real identity separate from his online identity. Admitting he was the kidnapping victim would connect him to the identifiable incident, so he reassured them without explaining his absence.

**翻译与边界说明**

Japanese names neutralized. English rendering retains online/offline ambiguity without relying on language-specific wordplay. No invented obligation to specify newspaper vs television.

## JP003 — 田中の着膨れしない暴漢対策

[日语来源](https://late-late.jp/mondai/show/17998)；作者：ダニー。

**English opening**

After being mugged, a company president avoided carrying large sums of cash. An unavoidable situation made him put one million yen in a bag. Worried that criminals would attack if they discovered it contained one million yen, he added more cash. Why?

**English solution (private)**

Kidnappers demanded fifty million yen for his daughter. To save money, he prepared bundles largely made of blank paper, with real notes on their outer faces: only one million yen was genuine. He feared violence if the deception was discovered. He replaced the top bundles with genuine banknotes, increasing the actual cash so a superficial inspection would be less likely to expose the fake ransom.

**翻译与边界说明**

Yen amounts retained; no currency conversion or title pun. Full causal account required, exact paper-sheet counts and handoff details not invented or required.

## JP004 — Youか一億円か

[日语来源](https://late-late.jp/mondai/show/10912)；作者：Nimie。

**English opening**

Over a smartphone, a daughter begs for a life to be spared and a kidnapper shouts: bring one hundred million yen alone by noon tomorrow, or this hostage dies; no police. Hearing the demand, her father decides the ransom should not be paid. Why?

**English solution (private)**

The father, not the daughter, is the hostage. The wealthy daughter receives the call and pleads for her father's life. Hearing her ordered to deliver the ransom alone, he believes the kidnapper will kill both of them to silence witnesses once she arrives. He tells her not to pay or come, sacrificing his own safety to protect his only family.

**翻译与边界说明**

Role-neutral threat retained in English rather than adding a gendered hostage pronoun. Japanese title pun omitted from solver. Source opening says 100 million yen but long solution says 1 billion; normalize to opening amount and do not grade exact amount.

## JP005 — ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ

[日语来源](https://late-late.jp/mondai/show/9571)；作者：本次抓取未能核实，留空。

**English opening**

In a newly settled village in Meiji-era Japan, a father finds a brown bear eating his only daughter in the forest. He shoots it and weeps over her remains. Knowing these wary bears rarely attack unfamiliar living humans, he nevertheless fears other villagers will also suffer having relatives eaten. Why?

**English solution (private)**

Days earlier an epidemic killed his daughter and several villagers. The new settlement lacked a crematorium and could not spare enough fuel, so their bodies were buried in the forest. On his daily visit to her grave, he discovered that a bear had dug her up and eaten her remains. He feared other bears would raid the graves and eat other families' dead relatives; he was not predicting attacks on living people.

**翻译与边界说明**

Graphic injury description compressed; causal facts retained. Bear behaviour is a premise of this fictional story, not a universal zoological claim. No kanji distinction or name meaning is needed.

## JP006 — 不敗者

[日语来源](https://late-late.jp/mondai/show/21352)；作者：アカシアン。

**English opening**

Kameda and Umio had been engaged in a struggle. Kameda was reduced to battered fragments, while Umio showed no damage. Yet people comparing them concluded that Umio was probably the loser. Why?

**English solution (private)**

Researchers were comparing their excavated skeletal remains, including photographs used in a history lecture. The culture cremated its dead. King Kameda's fragmented bones came from a proper cremation and royal burial. Duke Umio's intact skeleton had instead been buried without that honour. The accepted historical explanation was that Kameda's agents poisoned him and secretly disposed of his body. His intact remains indicated defeat and improper burial, not survival or victory.

**翻译与边界说明**

Short English translation/paraphrase. Title is not shown to solver; no pun needed. Keep archaeological inference distinct from certainty and distinguish death before observation from condition during political struggle.
