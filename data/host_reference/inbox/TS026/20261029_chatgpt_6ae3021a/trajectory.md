"One stab, two stabs, three stabs."

"Silly, don't cry!"

But because the narrator is an onion, it cannot speak aloud. It can only shout those words in its imagination.

The central irony is that the onion is concerned about the very person cutting it, without realizing that its own chemical defenses are causing his tears.

All the unusual details are explained by the story being told from the imagined perspective of an onion.
