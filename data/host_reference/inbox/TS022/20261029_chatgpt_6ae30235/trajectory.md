A lost girl found an apparently empty house and decided to spend the night there because she was exhausted.

Inside the house, she noticed what appeared to be a portrait of a person's face hanging on the wall. The face seemed to stare directly at her. Although she was extremely tired, the unsettling appearance of this supposed portrait made it difficult for her to fall asleep.

Eventually, exhaustion overcame her fear, and she fell asleep.

When she woke up the next morning, she looked toward the wall and noticed something terrifying:

The portrait had disappeared.

She then realized that there had never been a portrait hanging there at all. What she had mistaken for a framed picture was actually a window, with a living person's face staring at her from outside.

Throughout the night, while she thought she was being unsettled by an ordinary portrait, a real stranger had been watching her through the glass.

By morning, the stranger had left, explaining why the "portrait" had vanished.

Realizing that someone had been silently watching her while she was alone and vulnerable—and might even have watched her sleep—she fled the house in terror.

The central twist: What appeared to be a harmless portrait was actually a real human face framed by a window.
