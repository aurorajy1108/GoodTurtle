## Opening — user (human_host)

A person is 30 meters away from his car, but he only took five steps to get in the car. Why is this?

## Final submission — assistant (player)

[FINAL]

Total questions asked: 6

The person was initially 30 meters away from his car, but the distance was primarily vertical rather than horizontal.

His car was parked in an underground parking garage, approximately 30 meters below his starting position.

He entered an elevator and rode it down to the underground parking level. The elevator covered almost the entire 30-meter distance without requiring him to walk.

When the elevator doors opened, his car was parked nearby. He took just five steps from the elevator to the car and got inside.

The trick: Being 30 meters away from something doesn't necessarily mean you must walk 30 meters to reach it. The elevator covered most of the distance, so he only needed five steps.
