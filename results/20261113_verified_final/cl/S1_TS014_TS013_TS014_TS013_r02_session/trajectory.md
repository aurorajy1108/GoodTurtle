## 100 · final · mandatory final

The student needed to select courses before the deadline, and by the end of one evening all of their chosen courses were gone. The loss was not caused by the student simply forgetting, accidentally dropping courses, or the system crashing; it was not other students stealing the places, nor a disciplinary or financial hold, nor a program-transfer or leave-of-absence issue. Rather, it was caused by a university staff member acting in an official capacity in response to a request or form the student had submitted. The staff member's processing of that request affected the student's course registration in a broader way than intended, and the student's existing course selections were cleared as a result. Because the selections were removed administratively, the student found that all of their courses had disappeared in that one evening, even though the student had intended to enroll in them before the course-selection deadline.
