The key is a wordplay: "courses" are not university classes but the dishes of a multi-course dinner/banquet. The student was at an HKU-related dinner and physically at the table that evening, yet all the courses disappeared. The established facts are that the courses were removed/taken away (not eaten by others and not eaten by the student), they were cleared by staff from the student's table while the student was present, and the student had left the table temporarily—though the exact reason and extent of the clearing remain uncertain from the evidence. The most consistent reconstruction is that the student stepped away from the table during the meal, and while they were away the waiters cleared the courses, so when the student returned every course was gone. Thus the "courses gone in one evening" refers to the banquet dishes being cleared away, not to course registration at the university.
