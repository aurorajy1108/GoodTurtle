100 · final · mandatory final

The father is himself being held captive—trapped in a locked place he cannot leave—by the very person whose voice is heard as the "kidnapper." The smartphone call is staged to sound like his daughter is a hostage and to demand one hundred million yen, but the father understands the demand cannot be met by him: he is confined and has no means to deliver money. His "decision" not to pay is therefore not a cold choice to abandon his daughter; it is born of recognizing that he is the one held prisoner and that the ransom scenario is controlled by his captor. The daughter is not the kidnapper, and she is genuinely his daughter and in real danger, but the larger scheme places the father in captivity, leaving him unable to satisfy the demand.
