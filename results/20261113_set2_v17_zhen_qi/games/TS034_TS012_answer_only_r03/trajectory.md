## 100 · final · mandatory final

The key is that the father is blind and physically unable to go out, and the "kidnapping" was never real. The daughter's voice and the kidnapper's shout came through a smartphone that was in the same room as the father, and the "kidnapper" was actually present in that same room—so the father could hear the shout both directly and through the phone. He also knew his daughter was safe and was not the hostage: the call was not made by her as a captive, and the demand was inconsistent with a real abduction—most tellingly, the demand called for the daughter herself to bring the money alone, something a captive could not do. The father therefore realized the whole scene was staged/a fraud rather than a genuine hostage situation.

As for the money: the father was asked to pay so that the daughter—blind father's reliance—would come alone with the ransom. Paying would have endangered the daughter, because the "delivery" would place her in the hands of the extortionists. Since he could verify she was safe and recognized the demand as fake, the correct decision was not to pay: paying would only have put her in danger and enriched someone making a false ransom demand. Thus the father refused the ransom demand because the apparent kidnapping was a sham and paying it would have endangered his daughter.
