## 100 · final · mandatory final

He concludes that obeying that condition would be dangerous and futile: it would put him under the kidnapper’s control, prevent any outside rescue, and leave both father and daughter at the kidnapper’s mercy. He therefore decides that the ransom should not be paid on those terms, preferring that the authorities be able to act rather than following the kidnapper’s isolated-drop instruction.
