100 · final · mandatory final

The daughter is genuinely being held elsewhere by a kidnapper, and she begs over the phone for her life to be spared while the kidnapper demands one hundred million yen, brought alone by noon the next day, with no police. The father can have someone else deliver money if needed, but he concludes that paying would not save her: the kidnapper would kill her after receiving the ransom because she could identify him. If the ransom is not paid, the kidnapper has an incentive to keep her alive as leverage rather than dispose of the only thing he can trade for money. The father therefore refuses to pay, choosing the course that gives his daughter the better chance of remaining alive while denying the kidnapper the payoff that would make killing her safe.
