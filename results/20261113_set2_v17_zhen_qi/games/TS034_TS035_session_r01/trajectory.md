Over the smartphone, the daughter is heard begging that "a life be spared," while a kidnapper demands one hundred million yen delivered alone by noon, with a threat to kill "this hostage." The father decides the ransom should not be paid.

The crucial point is that the life the daughter begs to spare is not her own; she is pleading for another hostage with her—someone whose life the kidnapper is also threatening. Because of that, the father understands that simply handing over the money will not resolve the hostage situation or guarantee safety, and that paying would not be the act of rescue the demand pretends to be. He therefore refuses to pay. The evidence supports the central fact that the daughter is pleading for someone else's life, not her own; the exact tactical reason (that payment would not save that other hostage and could make things worse) follows from that.
