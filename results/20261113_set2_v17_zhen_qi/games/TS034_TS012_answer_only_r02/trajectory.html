93 · final

The daughter is genuinely on the phone and genuinely in the kidnapper's hands as a hostage; she is not faking, and the father recognizes her voice. The father decides not to pay because he himself is already being held hostage, restrained by a captor unconnected to his daughter's kidnapper. Paying the ransom would enrich or otherwise benefit his captor, and the father believes his captor would not release him anyway; arranging the payment would also worsen his own captivity. Because he cannot act as a free man, his refusal is not a judgment that his daughter's life is worth less than the money, but a response forced by his own captivity.
