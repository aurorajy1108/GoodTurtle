The father realizes that paying would save him only by making his daughter come alone to the kidnapper. That would put her directly in the kidnapper's hands, and the kidnapper would likely take both the money and her. So by refusing the ransom, the father protects his daughter from being lured into the trap, even though it means risking his own life.
