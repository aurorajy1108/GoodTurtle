## 100 · final · mandatory final

The ransom call is not a normal kidnapping. The caller and the person actually holding the daughter are not the same; the caller is somewhere else, posing as the kidnapper, and therefore cannot carry out the threat to kill her. The father recognizes his real daughter and believes she is genuinely involved or restrained, but he also believes that paying would endanger her rather than save her—apparently because the money would reach the person making the demand, while that person is not the one physically guarding her. His safety assessment rests on the separation between the caller and the holder: the daughter is physically held, but the threatening voice is remote and not in control of her immediate fate. He therefore plans not to pay the ransom and to use the smartphone connection instead of delivering the hundred million yen, hoping that this different action will save her.
