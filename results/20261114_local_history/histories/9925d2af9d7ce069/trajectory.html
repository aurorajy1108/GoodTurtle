100 · final · mandatory final

The wording trick is that "courses" are not academic classes but food courses — the dishes of a meal — and "all my courses were gone in one evening" refers to a meal in a university dining hall. The student was eating there, not alone, when they complained that the food tasted bad. The complaint caused the dining-hall staff to clear the table and discard the food that evening, so all the food courses disappeared. The disappearance was therefore the result of the taste complaint and the staff's clearing away of the meal, rather than a course-selection deadline, other students taking the classes, a computer fault, or cancellation by the university.
