At school, A deliberately told the authorities about a murder B’s parent had already committed. The parent faced criminal consequences, and B moved in with a relative, prompting B to transfer schools. The crime and its revelation contributed to B’s shame about having a parent who had committed murder and fear of others’ judgment. Years later, after A and B had reunited, married, and seemed loving, B voluntarily disappeared partly for those reasons. A had not killed B. Hoping B would reappear and tell the authorities the confession was false—and hoping to comfort and support B—A falsely claimed to have killed them. That statement led authorities to suspect and arrest A.
