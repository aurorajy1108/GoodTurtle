## 97 · final

Afterward, the victorious authorities deliberately denied Umio the customary funeral rites and buried him. Kameda, by contrast, was cremated as part of his ordinary funeral rites, leaving his remains fragmented. People therefore saw Umio’s intact body not as a sign of victory, but as evidence that he had been denied the funeral treatment given to the victor—and concluded that he was probably the loser.
