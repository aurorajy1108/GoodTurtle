96 · final

The student was excellent at tests and exams, so their individual work would normally earn high marks. This course, however, included a group project worth enough of the course grade that it alone determined pass/fail. The student was placed in a group with a classmate who did literally no work on the project. Because the project received a single shared group grade rather than individually assessed contributions, the classmate's total lack of contribution dragged that shared grade down to a failing level. The failing group-project grade was then applied to the student's course grade — and since the project weight alone was enough to drop the overall average below passing, the student failed the course despite having done all the work alone and excelling in every individual test and exam. Thus a smart student who always passes failed this course because they were graded on a shared group project sabotaged by a non-contributing classmate, not because of any error, illness, cheating, or their own mistake.
