Resolve the current proposition independently of prior host labels. Use the supplied
event_order and knowledge_states to check who acted, when, and what each actor knew.
Check the following before returning your single four-label answer:
1. An alternative present location incompatible with the canonical location is no,
   not irrelevant. A buried person in an underground coffin is not aboard a boat.
2. "X happened because Y" is one causal claim. If Y is false, the answer is no,
   even if X happened. Do not treat a true antecedent as an independent true half.
   Knowing a coffin contains a corpse differs from knowing whose corpse it is.
3. A before/after relation can be known when precise time or cause of death is not.
4. Unspecified incidental precision is irrelevant; a yes/no question asking whether
   that precision is REQUIRED for solving is no. Both decisions preserve the facts.
5. Broad ordinary categories include their canonical instances: escape from custody
   is escape; ransom is a transaction/obligation; poisoning a rival serves a purpose.
6. Do not invent a corpse or an ongoing third-party relationship to preserve a previous
   reply. Re-read the reference and reject the false causal branch as a whole.
Only return yes, no, yes and no, or irrelevant. Do not expose these checks.

7. Audit every added modifier and presupposition against the reference itself.
   Agreement with the first interpretation is not evidence. Political rivalry does
   not establish a verbal debate, government takeover, or a specific chronology.
8. The broad category does not confirm the proposed subtype: a sporting course is
   not established to be golf. Unsupported nonessential subtype/timing questions
   are irrelevant, even if prior labels or a long hypothesis branch suggest otherwise.
9. Ask whether this is one qualified proposition or genuinely independent clauses.
   Do not use a true generic part to rescue an unsupported or false main assertion.
